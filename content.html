When writing HTML, you may need to escape certain characters to ensure they are displayed correctly in the browser. This may be because there isn't a direct way to represent them in the HTML source, or because the character has a special meaning in HTML.

This is often done with a set of characters beginning with with an ampersand & and finishing with a semicolon ;. The table below lists some common HTML character escapes in the context of text:

Character Escape Sequence
< &lt;
> &gt;
" &quot;
' &#39;
& &amp;
© &copy;
® &reg;
™ &trade;
— &mdash;
